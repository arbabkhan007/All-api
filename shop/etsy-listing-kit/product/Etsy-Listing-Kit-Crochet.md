## The three numbers that matter

| Field | Etsy's limit | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Title | 140 characters | Etsy weights the first 40 characters most heavily, and mobile cuts titles off at about 45–55 |
| Tags | 13 tags of 20 characters | Each tag is an independent chance to match a buyer search. An empty slot is a search you never appear in |
| Description | 10,000 characters | Only the first 160 characters show before "read more" |

---

## The whole kit in eight rules

1. A tag is 20 characters maximum. Spaces and punctuation count.
2. Use all 13 tags. There is no penalty for filling every slot.
3. Multi-word phrases only. A single word like `art` or `plaid` is too broad to ever rank.
4. Never repeat a tag. Etsy already reads the words across your tags and mixes them.
5. Cover five angles: what it is, a synonym, style, occasion, audience.
6. Don't spend tags on your category or attributes. Those already act like tags.
7. Title: primary keyword in the first 40 characters.
8. Description: the first 160 characters say what it is and who it's for.

# Chapter 1 · Tags — the 20-character rule

## The rule

**13 tags per listing. 20 characters per tag. Spaces and punctuation count.**

Etsy's tag field simply refuses anything past 20 characters, so an over-long tag is a tag you cannot use at all.

## What counts as a character

| Character | Counts | Example |
|---|:--:|---|
| Letters | Yes | boho = 4 |
| Spaces | Yes | crochet tree = 12 |
| Hyphens | Yes | no-sew = 6 |
| Ampersands | Yes | art & decor = 11 |
| Numbers | Yes | planner 2026 = 12 |

Rule of thumb: 20 characters is roughly two to four short words.

## Multi-word beats single-word

| Weak tag | Why it fails | Strong replacement |
|---|---|---|
| art | Far too broad | crochet tree |
| pattern | Drowns in competition | crochet pattern |
| gift | No purchase intent | gift for crocheters |
| christmas | One word, no intent | christmas decor |
| uk | Not a search phrase | uk crochet terms |

Buyers search in phrases, so tags should be phrases.

## The split technique

When a phrase is longer than 20 characters, split it into two tags instead of mangling it. Etsy recombines words across your tags, so you keep the full meaning.

| Too long | Chars | Becomes | Plus |
|---|---:|---|---|
| crochet christmas tree pattern | 30 | crochet pattern (15) | christmas tree (14) |
| no sew crochet pattern pdf | 25 | no sew pattern (14) | crochet pattern (15) |
| crochet tree skirt pattern | 25 | tree skirt (10) | crochet pattern (15) |
| christmas ornament crochet | 25 | crochet ornament (15) | christmas ornament (17) |
| uk and us crochet terms | 23 | uk crochet terms (16) | us crochet terms (16) |

## The five-angle framework

| Angle | Slots | Question it answers | Example |
|---|---:|---|---|
| What it is | 3–4 | What did the buyer just find? | crochet wreath |
| Synonym | 2–3 | What else is it called? | christmas decor |
| Style | 2–3 | How does it look? | farmhouse christmas |
| Occasion | 2–3 | When or why is it bought? | front door decor |
| Audience | 1–2 | Who is it for? | gift for crocheters |

## Don't waste a slot

| Skip it | Because |
|---|---|
| Words already in your category | Etsy treats the category and every parent category as tags already |
| Words already in your attributes | Colour, material, occasion and style attributes act as tags too |
| Exact copies of your title | Partial overlap is fine; exact duplication adds nothing |
| Your shop name | Nobody searches it, and it costs you a slot |
| Comma-stuffed tags | One tag field is one phrase. `art, print, poster` is one broken tag, not three |

# Chapter 3 · Descriptions

## The two-job structure

**The first 160 characters do the ranking.** That is what shows before "read more", and it is read by Etsy search, Google and AI shopping assistants. It has to say what the product is and who it is for, in buyer language.

**Everything after that does the selling** — it answers the questions a buyer would otherwise message you about.

| Section | Length | Job |
|---|---:|---|
| Hook | 160 chars | What it is plus who it is for |
| What you get | 3–6 bullets | Files, sizes, formats |
| Skill level | 2–4 lines | Who can make it, and what's explained |
| Materials | 5–8 bullets | Yarn, hook, notions |
| Licence | 2 lines | What the buyer may do with it |
| Safety | 3–5 lines | The honest limits of the finished item |
| Care | 2 lines | Cleaning and storage |
| FAQ | 4–6 lines | Kill the top objections |

## What every crochet pattern listing must state

- File formats included, and that it is a digital PDF with nothing physical shipped
- Finished dimensions in centimetres and inches
- Yarn weights and amounts in grams
- Hook size, and the size that gives the right fabric
- Skill level and what the buyer needs to know already
- Whether the pattern is US terms, UK terms, or both
- Licence: what may be made, what may be sold, and the credit required
- Honest safety limits for the finished item
- Care instructions that match what you have actually tested
