# 04 · Description Template — 10,000 characters

## The two-job structure

**The first ~160 characters do the ranking.** That's what shows before "read more", and it's read by Etsy search, Google, and AI shopping assistants — so it has to say what the product is and who it's for, in buyer language. Don't copy your title word for word [1](https://www.listadum.com/blog/how-to-write-good-product-descriptions-for-etsy).

**Everything after that does the selling** — it answers the questions a buyer would otherwise message you about.

| Section | Length | Job |
|---|---:|---|
| Hook | 160 chars | What it is + who it's for |
| What you get | 3–6 bullets | Files, sizes, formats |
| How it works | 2–4 lines | Instant-download steps |
| Good to know | 2–4 lines | Licence, printing, edits |
| FAQ | 3–5 lines | Kill the top objections |

## Fill-in template

```
[HOOK — 160 chars max. What this is and who it's for, in the words a buyer
would type into Etsy search.]

WHAT YOU GET
• [Number] [file type] files — [sizes included, e.g. 5"x7", 8"x10", 11"x14"]
• [Format list: JPG / PDF / PNG / SVG / DXF]
• [Resolution, e.g. 300 DPI, print-ready]
• [What is editable vs fixed]
• [Bonus file, if any]

HOW IT WORKS
1. Buy this listing.
2. Download instantly from your Etsy Purchases page — no waiting on email.
3. Open the [PDF / ZIP] and print at home, at a print shop, or upload to [platform].

GOOD TO KNOW
• This is a digital download — no physical item ships.
• Colors vary slightly between screens and printers.
• Files are for [personal use / small commercial use — choose one].
• Need a different size? Message me, it's usually free.

FAQ
Q: Can I edit the text?
A: [Yes, in Canva / No, this design is fixed — state it plainly.]

Q: What size should I print?
A: [Recommendation, and note that files scale to the ratios listed above.]

Q: Do you take custom requests?
A: [Yes — link or say how to ask / No.]
```

## Writing rules

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Lead with what it is and who it's for | Open with "Handmade with love!" |
| Use short paragraphs and bullets | Write one dense block of text |
| State formats, sizes, DPI, editability | Leave specs to guesswork |
| Repeat your main keyword 4–8 times naturally | Stuff keywords in a row |
| Answer the questions buyers message you about | End with "Buy now!!!" |

---

## Digital-download specifics buyers always need

- **File formats included** (JPG, PNG, PDF, SVG, DXF, Canva link)
- **Dimensions and resolution** (e.g. 300 DPI, 2:3 and 4:5 ratios)
- **What is editable vs fixed**
- **Instant-download steps** and how to open the files
- **Printing recommendations** (home printer vs print shop)
- **Licence and usage rights**
- **Support and contact info** [1](https://www.listadum.com/blog/how-to-write-good-product-descriptions-for-etsy)
