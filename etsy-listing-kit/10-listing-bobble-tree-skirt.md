### Full description — copy and paste

```
Download the PDF and crochet a bobble snowflake tree skirt: a flat twelve-spoke circle
with a closed centre ring, in mini, standard and large — US and UK terms.

This is a DIGITAL CROCHET PATTERN (PDF) — no finished skirt is shipped.

WHAT YOU GET
• One PDF pattern, instant download — start today
• Every round written in US terms AND the exact UK equivalent, side by side
• Three sizes from one twelve-column formula:
  - Mini / tabletop — stop after R14 — 18-21 in (46-53 cm) across
  - Standard — stop after R23 — 29-33 in (74-84 cm) across
  - Large — stop after R32 — 38-43 in (97-109 cm) across
• A closed centre ring about 1.5-2 in unstretched, so the skirt slips over the stand
  before the tree goes up — start with ch 24 for a larger opening
• Scalloped border: 28 scallops at mini, 46 at standard, 64 at large — all three
  endings are divisible by 6, so the border closes cleanly at every size
• Optional contrast bobbles and 12 surface slip-stitch snowflake spokes
• Design Code NS 14 — (c) 2026 Novality Store

SKILL LEVEL: EASY - INTERMEDIATE
You should be comfortable with double crochet, joined rounds and chain spaces. The
pattern explains the twelve-spoke maths — round N always ends at exactly 12 x N
stitches — plus the 5-dc bobble, changing colour cleanly at a join, and surface
slip-stitch spokes. Worked in closed rounds that are joined, never turned; the ch 2 at
the start of a round never counts as a stitch.

WHY IT LIES FLAT
Twelve increase columns add 12 stitches every round, and every third round from R5 is a
bobble round, so the bobbles bloom in regular rings as the circle grows. The pattern
gives the exact count rule so you can verify or extend any round yourself.

MATERIALS NEEDED
• Worsted or aran (#4) yarn — 700-1,200 g for the standard or large skirt
• 5-5.5 mm hook
• Optional contrast yarn for the bobble rounds and surface lines
• Stitch marker, scissors, tapestry needle

COLOURWAYS
Forest & cream · Snow white & red scallops · Navy & silver · Single-colour cream

HELPFUL EXTRAS IN THE PATTERN
Troubleshooting for a skirt that ruffles like a lettuce leaf, one that cups like a bowl,
and bobbles that will not pop — plus the count rule for adding rounds, and a reminder to
measure the lying-flat skirt against your tree stand before committing to a border.

LICENCE
Make as many skirts as you like for yourself, gifts or charity. You may also sell
finished physical skirts in small quantities, with credit to "Novality Store". The
pattern itself may not be copied, shared, resold, translated or uploaded.

SAFETY
This design is intended as home decor, not a toy, and it is NOT FLAMEPROOF. Keep the
finished skirt away from open flames, heaters and hot lamps, and use only cool-running
lights that are approved for the tree and location. No finished skirt has been
independently assessed by Novality Store for flammability or another product-safety
requirement; the finished-item maker or seller is responsible for all market-specific
assessment, testing, labelling and traceability duties before supply.

CARE AND STORAGE
Follow the actual yarn label. Before publishing any cleaning claim, wash and dry a
full-size sample by the proposed method, then remeasure the diameter and centre opening
and check for dye transfer, shrinkage, bobble distortion and stretched joins. Until that
test passes, make no machine-wash or tumble-dry claim. Store completely dry and away
from heat, flame, damp and pests; lay flat or roll rather than sharply creasing the
bobble design.

FAQ
Q: Is this a finished tree skirt?
A: No — this listing is for the digital PDF pattern only. Nothing physical ships.

Q: US or UK terms?
A: Both. Every round appears twice, US on the left and the UK equivalent on the right.
Stitch counts are identical either way.

Q: Which size should I make?
A: Mini for a tabletop tree, standard for most full-size trees, large for a wide or
very full tree. Measure the lying-flat skirt against your stand before adding the
border — yarn, hook and tension all change the diameter.

Q: Will the centre opening fit my stand?
A: The ring is about 1.5-2 in unstretched. If your stand is wider, start with ch 24
instead of ch 20 for a larger opening.

Q: Can I sell the skirts I make?
A: Yes, physical finished items in small quantities, with credit to "Novality Store".

Happy crocheting. Tag your makes with #NovalityStore — lay it under the tree, plug in
the lights, and step back.
```
