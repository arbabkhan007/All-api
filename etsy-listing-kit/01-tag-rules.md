# 01 · Tag Rules — the 20-character limit

## The rule

> **13 tags per listing. 20 characters per tag. Spaces and punctuation count.**

| | Limit |
|---|---:|
| Tags per listing | 13 |
| Characters per tag | 20 |
| Recommended words per tag | 2–4 |

Etsy's tag field simply refuses anything past 20 characters, so an over-long tag is a tag you cannot use at all [2](https://typecount.com/blog/etsy-character-limit).

---

## What counts as a character

| Character | Counts? | Example |
|---|:--:|---|
| Letters | Yes | `boho` = 4 |
| Spaces | Yes | `boho wall art` = 13 |
| Hyphens | Yes | `print-at-home` = 13 |
| Ampersands | Yes | `art & decor` = 11 |
| Numbers | Yes | `planner 2026` = 12 |

**Rule of thumb:** 20 characters ≈ 2–4 short words.

---

## Multi-word beats single-word

| Weak tag | Why it fails | Strong replacement |
|---|---|---|
| `art` | Far too broad to ever rank | `boho wall art` (13) |
| `planner` | Drowns in competition | `undated planner` (15) |
| `svg` | Generic, non-specific | `cricut svg` (10) |
| `gift` | No purchase intent | `housewarming gift` (17) |
| `printable` | Not a search phrase | `printable poster` (16) |

Single-word tags are the most common tag mistake — buyers search in phrases, so tags should be phrases [3](https://loveeattravelrepeat.com/etsy-tags-the-complete-guide-to-seo-visibility/).

---

## The split technique: fixing a tag that's too long

When your keyword phrase is longer than 20 characters, **split it into two tags** instead of shortening it into nonsense. Etsy recombines words across your tags, so you keep the full meaning [1](https://mockupmaestro.com/blog/marketplace-strategy/etsy-seo-guide-2026/).

| Too-long phrase | Chars | Becomes tag 1 | Chars | Becomes tag 2 | Chars |
|---|---:|---|---:|---|---:|
| printable wall art set of 3 | 27 | printable wall art | 18 | wall art set of 3 | 17 |
| digital planner for goodnotes | 29 | digital planner | 15 | goodnotes planner | 17 |
| editable canva template bundle | 30 | canva template | 14 | editable template | 17 |
| instant download digital file | 29 | instant download | 16 | digital file | 12 |
| boho nursery wall art print | 27 | boho nursery art | 16 | nursery wall art | 16 |
| svg cut files for cricut | 24 | svg cut file | 12 | cricut svg | 10 |
| personalized wedding invitation | 31 | wedding invitation | 18 | personalized pdf | 16 |
| small business budget planner | 29 | budget planner | 14 | small business | 14 |
| nursery wall art printable set | 30 | nursery wall art | 16 | printable wall art | 18 |

## The five-angle framework

A strong 13-tag set spreads across five angles rather than saying the same thing thirteen ways [5](https://metadatareactor.com/blog/etsy-seo-complete-guide/):

| Angle | Slots | Question it answers | Example |
|---|---:|---|---|
| What it is | 3–4 | What did the buyer just find? | `boho wall art` |
| Synonym | 2–3 | What else is it called? | `digital wall decor` |
| Style / aesthetic | 2–3 | How does it look? | `scandinavian decor` |
| Occasion / use | 2–3 | When or why is it bought? | `housewarming gift` |
| Audience / platform | 1–2 | Who is it for? | `goodnotes planner` |

---

## Don't waste a slot

| Skip it | Because |
|---|---|
| Words already in your category | Etsy treats the category and every parent category as tags already [3](https://loveeattravelrepeat.com/etsy-tags-the-complete-guide-to-seo-visibility/) |
| Words already in your attributes | Colour, material, occasion and style attributes act as tags too [3](https://loveeattravelrepeat.com/etsy-tags-the-complete-guide-to-seo-visibility/) |
| Exact copies of your title | Partial overlap is fine; exact duplication adds nothing [1](https://mockupmaestro.com/blog/marketplace-strategy/etsy-seo-guide-2026/) |
| Your shop name | Nobody searches it, and it costs you a slot |
| Comma-separated keyword stuffing | One tag field = one phrase. `art, print, poster` is one broken tag, not three |

---

## After publishing

Tags are not set-and-forget. Check Etsy Stats after 2–4 weeks and replace tags that bring no impressions, then give the listing another 2–4 weeks before judging again [1](https://www.insightagent.app/guides/keywords-for-etsy-listing).
