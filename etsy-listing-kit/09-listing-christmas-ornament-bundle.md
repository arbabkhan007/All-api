### Full description — copy and paste

```
Download the PDF and crochet three quick Christmas ornaments from leftover yarn: a
stuffed bauble, a blocked star and a lacy snowflake, in US and UK terms.

This is a DIGITAL CROCHET PATTERN (PDF) — no finished ornaments are shipped.

WHAT YOU GET
• One PDF containing THREE patterns: round bauble, five-point star, six-arm snowflake
• Every round written in US terms AND the exact UK equivalent, side by side
• Stitch counts for every round of all three designs
• Finished sizes: bauble about 3.5-4 cm (1.5 in) across, star about 9 cm (3.5 in),
  snowflake about 10 cm (4 in)
• Sizes scale with yarn — use DK for dainty tree ornaments, chunky for bold window
  pieces
• Design Code NS 13 — (c) 2026 Novality Store

TIME AND YARN PER PIECE
• Bauble — about 25 minutes, 8-10 g of yarn — tree ornament or garland
• Star — about 20 minutes, 6-8 g — gift topper or garland
• Snowflake — about 15 minutes, 4-6 g — window, gift tag or garland

Allow 15-40 minutes each including finishing; blocking and drying add elapsed time.

SKILL LEVEL: BEGINNER
You should be comfortable with chains, slip stitch, single crochet, increases and
decreases. The pattern explains the rest step by step: the magic ring, the star's
ch-2 fan points, working into chain spaces instead of individual chains, and how to
block each piece so it holds its shape.

THREE DESIGNS, ONE GARLAND
Because all three are small and quick, they string neatly on one length of yarn — make
a matching garland by repeating all three patterns along it.

MATERIALS NEEDED
• Small amounts (5-15 g) of worsted / aran or DK yarn in white, red, gold, green or
  silver. Cotton gives the crispest snowflake; acrylic works too.
• 3-4 mm hook (3 mm for DK, 4 mm for worsted)
• A pinch of fibre fill for the bauble; optional metallic yarn for surface stripes
• Tapestry needle, scissors, and a pin board or mat for blocking the star and snowflake

COLOURWAYS
Snow white · Classic red & white · Gold · Evergreen trio · Frost blue

HELPFUL EXTRAS IN THE PATTERN
Troubleshooting for a bauble that looks like a lemon, a star that won't lie flat and
snowflake arms that lean — plus a two-colour bauble option, blocking guidance for
cotton and acrylic, and a tip for threading each finished loop through a backing card
for gifting.

LICENCE
Make as many ornaments as you like for yourself, gifts or charity. You may also sell
finished physical ornaments in small batches, in shops, markets and online, as long as
credit is given to "Novality Store". The pattern itself may not be copied, shared,
resold, translated or uploaded. Mass production requires written permission.

SAFETY
These designs are intended as decorations, not toys. Keep them and their hanging loops
out of children's reach, avoid beads, loose bells and glued additions, secure every
loop twice, and make each loop only as long as needed for its intended support. No
finished ornament has been shown by Novality Store to comply with ASTM F963, EN 71 or
another product-safety regime, so please do not market one as compliant or suitable
for children.

CARE AND STORAGE
Follow the yarn and metallic-thread labels. Test any cleaning method on a complete
blocked sample first and check for dye transfer, distortion and weakened hanging loops
once it is fully dry. Store the ornaments dry and flat or supported, without crushing
the star or snowflake points. Reblock only with the fibre-appropriate method, and never
apply an iron directly.

FAQ
Q: Is this a finished set of ornaments?
A: No — this listing is for the digital PDF pattern only. Nothing physical ships.

Q: US or UK terms?
A: Both. Every round appears twice, US on the left and the UK equivalent on the right.
Stitch counts are identical either way.

Q: Do I have to block the star and snowflake?
A: Yes, blocking is what makes them crisp. Wet or mist cotton and pin every point.
Acrylic often will not hold a shape from pins alone — if the yarn label permits, steam
cautiously from above without letting the iron or steamer touch the fabric, and test a
scrap first.

Q: Can I sell the ornaments I make?
A: Yes, physical finished items in small batches, with credit to "Novality Store".

Happy crocheting. Tag your makes with #NovalityStore — tag us in your trees and tables.
```
