### Full description — copy and paste

```
Download the PDF and crochet a soft bobble Christmas tree this weekend: a one-piece
pattern with tiered bobbles, written in US and UK crochet terms.

This is a DIGITAL CROCHET PATTERN (PDF) — no finished tree is shipped.

WHAT YOU GET
• One PDF pattern, instant download — start crocheting today
• Every round written in US terms AND the exact UK equivalent, side by side
• Round-by-round instructions with stitch counts for all 26 rounds
• Finished size: about 12 cm (4.7 in) tall and 7 cm (2.8 in) wide in worsted / aran
  on a 4 mm hook
• Large version: about 15-16 cm tall in chunky yarn on a 5 mm hook
• Make a set of three: DK + 3 mm (~9 cm), worsted + 4 mm (~12 cm), chunky + 5 mm
  (~15-16 cm) — the stitch counts are identical at every hook size
• Optional contrast bobbles in red, gold or cream for a baubles-on-a-tree look
• Design Code NS 12 — (c) 2026 Novality Store

SKILL LEVEL: EASY - INTERMEDIATE
You should be comfortable with chain, slip stitch, single crochet, increase and
decrease. The pattern explains the rest step by step: the magic ring, working in
continuous rounds, the back-loop-only round that folds the base upward, the 5-dc
bobble, and carrying a contrast colour for bauble bobbles. Make time is about 2-3
hours.

ONE PIECE — NO ASSEMBLY
The tree is worked from the base up in continuous rounds. A back-loop-only round
creates a fold ridge so the base turns up into the cone wall, bobble tiers alternate
with plain rounds, and a hidden decrease sits inside every bobble repeat so the cone
tapers evenly and the bobble columns stay straight. Only the tip needs closing.

A TREE THAT STANDS ON ITS OWN
The cone holds its shape on tension, so this tree needs only a light fill — stuff the
bottom half and keep the upper cone soft. An optional circle of firm felt or cardboard
placed inside the base adds extra stability for shelf display (trace just inside the
fold ridge and cut it 3-5 mm smaller).

MATERIALS NEEDED
• Worsted / aran (#4) yarn in green — 60-80 g
• Small amounts of contrast yarn for bauble bobbles (red, gold or cream)
• 4 mm (US G-6) hook — 3 mm for the DK mini, 5 mm for the chunky version
• Fibre fill about 25 g — a little only, this tree stands on tension
• Optional: one circle of firm felt or cardboard for the base disc
• Stitch marker, tapestry needle, scissors

COLOURWAYS
Fir green · Sage velvet · Snow white · Crimson berry · Gold-tipped

HELPFUL EXTRAS IN THE PATTERN
Troubleshooting for wandering bobble columns, a floppy tip and a base that won't sit
flat — plus finishing options: contrast bobble tiers, a hanging loop at the tip, and
how to work the set of three.

LICENCE
Make as many trees as you like for yourself, gifts or charity. You may also sell
finished physical trees in small batches, in shops, markets and online, as long as
credit is given to "Novality Store". The pattern itself may not be copied, shared,
resold, translated or uploaded. Mass production requires written permission.

SAFETY
As written, the bobbles are crocheted into the fabric and the design uses no beads or
glued decorations. Adding beads, bells or other embellishments can create detachable
small parts, so do not add them without reassessing the finished item. No finished tree
has been shown by Novality Store to comply with ASTM F963, EN 71 or another toy-safety
regime — please do not market one as "baby-safe" or compliant. Stuff firmly and secure
every yarn end.

CARE
Gentle surface clean with a damp cloth and dry upright away from heat. A tree
containing a cardboard base disc is not washable — keep it dry.

FAQ
Q: Is this a finished tree?
A: No — this listing is for the digital PDF pattern only. Nothing physical ships.

Q: US or UK terms?
A: Both. Every round appears twice, US on the left and the UK equivalent on the right.
Stitch counts are identical either way.

Q: Does it stand up by itself?
A: Yes. The cone holds its shape on tension with only a light fill, and the pattern
includes an optional felt or cardboard base disc if you want extra stability.

Q: Can I make it bigger or smaller?
A: Yes — use chunky yarn on a 5 mm hook for a 15-16 cm tree, or DK on a 3 mm hook for
a 9 cm mini. The stitch counts are the same at every size.

Q: Can I sell the trees I make?
A: Yes, physical finished items in small batches, with credit to "Novality Store".

Happy crocheting. Tag your makes with #NovalityStore — show us your forest.
```
