### Full description — copy and paste

```
Download the PDF and crochet a no-sew Christmas gnome this afternoon: a one-piece
amigurumi pattern with a bobble nose, written in US and UK crochet terms.

This is a DIGITAL CROCHET PATTERN (PDF) — no finished gnome is shipped.

WHAT YOU GET
• One PDF pattern, instant download — start crocheting today
• Every round written in US terms AND the exact UK equivalent, side by side
• Round-by-round instructions with stitch counts for every round
• Finished size: about 11-12.5 cm (4.5-5 in) tall, 5 cm (2 in) wide at the base
• Size variations included: mini ornament (8-9 cm) and large decoration (15 cm)
• Five colourways to choose from: Santa red, Forest green, Nordic grey, Candy pink,
  Midnight navy
• Design Code NS 11 — (c) 2026 Novality Store

SKILL LEVEL: BEGINNER
If you can chain, slip stitch, single crochet, increase and decrease, you can make this.
Everything else is explained step by step: the magic ring, working in a continuous
spiral, the 5-dc bobble nose, clean colour changes, and the invisible decrease for a
smooth hat. Make time is about 1-2 hours.

NO SEWING, NO ASSEMBLY
Body, beard, face and hat are worked as ONE continuous piece from the base up, with
simple colour changes along the way. There are no pieces to seam and nothing to sew
together at the end — only the final closing round.

MATERIALS NEEDED
• Worsted / aran (#4) yarn — body and hat 40-50 g in red or green
• White or fluffy white yarn for the beard — 10-15 g
• Skin-tone yarn — about 10 g
• 3.5 mm (US E-4) hook, or whatever size gives a dense fabric
• Fibre fill 20-30 g
• Stitch marker, tapestry needle, scissors
• Optional: 6-8 mm safety eyes, or dark yarn for embroidered eyes

HELPFUL EXTRAS IN THE PATTERN
Troubleshooting for slanting colour lines, a nose bobble that won't pop, and a gnome
that tips over — plus optional finishes: a white hat brim, extra beard texture, and a
hanging loop so it works as a tree ornament.

LICENCE
Make as many gnomes as you like for yourself, gifts or charity. You may also sell
finished physical gnomes in small batches, in shops, markets and online, as long as
credit is given to "Novality Store". The pattern itself may not be copied, shared,
resold, translated or uploaded. Mass production requires written permission.

SAFETY
This is not a certified toy. No finished gnome has been shown by Novality Store to
comply with ASTM F963, EN 71 or another toy-safety regime, so please do not market one
as "baby-safe" or compliant. If you use plastic safety eyes, lock the washers from the
inside before stuffing and closing. The maker or seller of the finished item is
responsible for all market-specific assessment, testing, labelling and traceability.

CARE
Gentle surface clean with a damp cloth and dry flat away from heat. Reshape the hat and
beard afterwards.

FAQ
Q: Is this a finished gnome?
A: No — this listing is for the digital PDF pattern only. Nothing physical ships.

Q: US or UK terms?
A: Both. Every round appears twice, US on the left and the UK equivalent on the right.
Stitch counts are identical either way.

Q: Do I have to sew pieces together?
A: No. The whole gnome is one piece worked from the base up; there is no assembly.

Q: Can I sell the gnomes I make?
A: Yes, physical finished items in small batches, with credit to "Novality Store".

Q: I'm stuck on a round. Can you help?
A: Message me — I'm happy to talk you through it.

Happy crocheting. Tag your makes with #NovalityStore — I love seeing gnome gatherings.
```
