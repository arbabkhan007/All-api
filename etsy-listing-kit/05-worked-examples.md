## What these three have in common

| Trait | Why it's there |
|---|---|
| Primary keyword in the first 40 chars | Etsy weights the start of the title most [1](https://mockupmaestro.com/blog/marketplace-strategy/etsy-seo-guide-2026/) |
| All 13 tags used, none repeated | Every slot is a separate search match [3](https://loveeattravelrepeat.com/etsy-tags-the-complete-guide-to-seo-visibility/) |
| No single-word tags | Single words are too broad to rank [3](https://loveeattravelrepeat.com/etsy-tags-the-complete-guide-to-seo-visibility/) |
| Tags spread across five angles | Broadens reach without duplicating the title [5](https://metadatareactor.com/blog/etsy-seo-complete-guide/) |
| Hook under 160 chars, in buyer language | That's all that shows before "read more" [2](https://typecount.com/blog/etsy-character-limit) |
| Formats and licence stated up front | Cuts the most common pre-purchase messages |
