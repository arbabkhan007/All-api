### Full description — copy and paste

```
Download the PDF and crochet an interchangeable wreath: a padded tube ring with three
removable decorations — a poinsettia, a snowflake or a bow — in US and UK terms.

This is a DIGITAL CROCHET PATTERN (PDF) — no finished wreath is shipped.

WHAT YOU GET
• One PDF pattern, instant download — start today
• Every round written in US terms AND the exact UK equivalent, side by side
• Three sizes, measured by TUBE LENGTH because round counts vary by gauge:
  - Mini ornament — 20-24 cm (8-9.5 in) tube — about 6.5-7.5 cm across
  - Standard door — 86-97 cm (34-38 in) tube — about 27-31 cm across
  - Large door — 119-132 cm (47-52 in) tube — about 38-42 cm across
• Three REMOVABLE decorations: poinsettia, lace snowflake and gift bow
• The centreline diameter is the tube length divided by 3.14; the finished outside
  diameter is one tube thickness larger, so measure your sample
• Design Code NS 15 — (c) 2026 Novality Store

SKILL LEVEL: BEGINNER
The tube is a constant 12 stitches — no increases, no decreases, round after round — so
the only skill the base really needs is single crochet in a spiral. The pattern explains
joining the tube end to end without twisting it, the snowflake arm, and the lark's-head
tie that makes each decoration removable.

ONE WREATH, THREE SEASONS
Every decoration carries its own folded tie, anchored under at least two sturdy stitches
on its back. Wrap the two tails in opposite directions around the tube and knot them at
the back. Untie to swap a poinsettia for a snowflake or a bow — nothing is sewn
permanently to the wreath, and no decoration has to be forced through its own attachment.

MATERIALS NEEDED
• Base: 150-200 g green worsted / aran (#4), 4 mm hook, fibre fill
• Decorations: small amounts of red, white, yellow and green yarn, optional metallic
  thread held with the snowflake yarn, 3.5-4 mm hook
• For a door wreath: a load-rated woven hanging cord or ribbon long enough to wrap
  twice around the full tube
• Stitch marker, scissors, tapestry needle

COLOURWAYS
Classic green & red · Winter white & gold · Berry wreath · Neutral farmhouse green

HELPFUL EXTRAS IN THE PATTERN
Troubleshooting for a tube that is stiff like a rope, a wreath that will not hold a
circle, and poinsettia petals that cluster on one side — plus stuffing guidance (little
and often, never at the end) and a mini 8-stitch ornament version.

LICENCE
Make as many wreaths as you like for yourself, gifts or charity. You may also sell
finished physical wreaths in small quantities, with credit to "Novality Store". The
pattern itself may not be copied, shared, resold, translated or uploaded.

SAFETY
This design is intended as home decor, not a toy. A stuffed wreath is heavier than it
looks: hang it from a securely installed, load-rated hook using a load-rated woven cord
or ribbon wrapped around the WHOLE tube, and do not rely on a suction cup or on a
decoration tie to carry the weight. Do not add loose or glued embellishments anywhere
children can reach, and keep the wreath away from open flames. No finished wreath or
hanging system has been independently load-tested or assessed by Novality Store; the
finished-item maker or seller is responsible for all market-specific assessment,
documentation, labelling and traceability duties before supply.

CARE AND STORAGE
Follow the labels for every yarn, stuffing, metallic thread and hanging component used.
Until a complete sample has been cleaned, dried, remeasured and inspected, recommend
gentle surface cleaning only — make no machine-wash claim. Store fully dry, supported
and uncompressed. Inspect the hanger, hook, tube seam and every tied decoration before
each season and after severe weather.

FAQ
Q: Is this a finished wreath?
A: No — this listing is for the digital PDF pattern only. Nothing physical ships.

Q: US or UK terms?
A: Both. Every round appears twice, US on the left and the UK equivalent on the right.
Stitch counts are identical either way.

Q: What size will fit my door?
A: Measure the tube, not the round count. The centreline diameter is the tube length
divided by 3.14, and the finished outside diameter is one tube thickness larger. The
standard door size uses an 86-97 cm tube.

Q: Do the decorations really come off?
A: Yes. Each one has its own tie knotted at the back of the wreath — untie it and swap
to a different decoration, or hang the plain green ring on its own.

Q: Can I sell the wreaths I make?
A: Yes, physical finished items in small quantities, with credit to "Novality Store".

Happy crocheting. Tag your makes with #NovalityStore — swap the decoration, swap the mood.
```
