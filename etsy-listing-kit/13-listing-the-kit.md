### Full description — copy and paste

```
Stop guessing at Etsy tags: a printable listing kit for crochet sellers, with
13-tag sets, title formulas and description templates that fit Etsy's limits.

This is a DIGITAL DOWNLOAD — a 24-page PDF guide plus a 4-page printable
worksheet pack. Nothing physical ships.

WHAT YOU GET
• A 24-page PDF guide, US Letter, instant download
• A 4-page printable worksheet pack — listing planner, tag planner, title
  planner, tag audit and price planner
• Five COMPLETE listings for crochet patterns, ready to copy and adapt:
  no-sew gnome, bobble Christmas tree, ornament bundle, bobble tree skirt and
  interchangeable wreath
• 65 tags already length-checked at 20 characters or fewer, so none of them
  will be rejected by Etsy
• Title formulas with the 140-character limit built in, and five fill-in templates
• A description skeleton built around the first 160 characters — the part Etsy
  actually shows before "read more"
• A chapter on what to charge, with observed competitor prices across four
  crochet categories
• A pre-publish checklist covering title, tags, description, files and photos

WHO IT IS FOR
Crochet pattern sellers who write their own listings. If you already know how to
crochet but not how Etsy search works, this is written for you.

WHAT IT IS NOT
This is not a keyword-volume tool and it does not connect to your shop. It is a
written system: the limits, the rules, and copy you can paste and adapt.

HOW TO USE IT
1. Read chapters 1 to 3 once — the tag rule, the title rule, the description rule.
2. Jump to chapter 4 and copy the listing closest to your product.
3. Fill in the worksheets before you publish.
4. Use the pricing chapter to set your price, then review it at 60 days.

WHY THE NUMBERS MATTER
Etsy gives you 140 characters of title, 13 tags of 20 characters each, and only
the first 160 characters of your description are visible before "read more".
Every template in this kit is built inside those limits.

LICENCE
Personal use in your own shop. Use every template, tag set and checklist to write
and publish your own listings, and keep the listings you write. You may not copy,
resell, share, redistribute, translate, publish or upload this kit or any part of
it, in digital or printed form, or claim it as your own.

FAQ
Q: Is this a physical book?
A: No. It is a digital PDF plus a printable worksheet pack. Nothing ships.

Q: Do I need any software?
A: A PDF reader is enough. The worksheets are designed to be printed.

Q: Is this about crochet only?
A: It is written for crochet pattern sellers, with crochet examples throughout.
The rules themselves apply to any digital listing on Etsy.

Q: Will this guarantee sales?
A: No listing tool can. What it does is make sure your copy fits Etsy's limits
and covers the keyword angles the algorithm reads.

Q: I'm brand new to Etsy. Is this too advanced?
A: No. Chapters 1 to 3 explain the limits from scratch, and chapter 4 gives you
finished copy to start from.
```
