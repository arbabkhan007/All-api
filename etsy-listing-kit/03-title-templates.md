# 03 · Title Templates — 140 characters

## The rules

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Hard limit | 140 characters [2](https://typecount.com/blog/etsy-character-limit) |
| Front-load | Your strongest keyword belongs in the **first 40 characters** [1](https://mockupmaestro.com/blog/marketplace-strategy/etsy-seo-guide-2026/) |
| Visible on mobile | Only the first ~45–55 characters show in search results [5](https://metadatareactor.com/blog/etsy-seo-complete-guide/) |
| Separators | Use `|` or `,` between phrases so humans can scan it in half a second |
| Casing | Title Case reads better in search results |
| Don't | Start with your shop name, or stuff one keyword five times |

| 6 | `Editable [Event] Invitation Template, [Style] [Event] Invite, [Extra] Card Template` | Event stationery |

## Weak vs strong

| Weak title | Why it fails | Strong version |
|---|---|---|
| Digital Art | Vague, wastes the 140 chars | Boho Wall Art Printable, Neutral Gallery Wall Set, Instant Download |
| Planner | One word, no buyer intent | Digital Planner 2026, GoodNotes Planner, Undated iPad Planner, Hyperlinked PDF |
| Cute SVG Files | No format, no machine, no project | SVG Bundle for Cricut, 50 Cut Files SVG DXF PNG, Vinyl Decal Designs |
| Wedding Stuff | Not a search phrase | Editable Wedding Invitation Template, Boho Wedding Invite, RSVP Card Set |
