## What you actually keep

Etsy's cut on a digital sale is smaller than on physical goods, but it is not zero. On a **$5 digital sale** you net roughly **$4.22** — $0.33 transaction fee, $0.40 payment processing, $0.05 regulatory, plus the $0.20 listing fee amortised across sales. Through offsite ads you net closer to **$3.50** [1](https://www.promptlesspress.com/blog-how-to-price-digital-products-etsy).

Approximate net per sale at the recommended prices:

| Price | Net (no offsite ads) | Net (offsite ads sale) |
|---:|---:|---:|
| $5.50 | ≈ $4.70 | ≈ $3.90 |
| $6.50 | ≈ $5.60 | ≈ $4.65 |
| $7.50 | ≈ $6.45 | ≈ $5.40 |
| $8.50 | ≈ $7.35 | ≈ $6.15 |
| $9.50 | ≈ $8.20 | ≈ $6.90 |

Two more costs to hold in mind: the **$0.20 listing renewal** every four months, and **offsite ads**, which are mandatory at 15% once a shop passes $10,000 in annual revenue.

## The rules this market imposes

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Price at or above the mid tier — $4+ minimum | Touch the $1–$3 tier; it's bulk/AI-generated bundles and it attracts refund-happy buyers |
| Use a struck-through "original" price for launch, then hold the real price | Leave a 50–75% discount running permanently — it trains buyers to never pay full price |
| Charge more for more content (3 sizes, 3 patterns, 3 motifs) | Price every pattern the same; a tree skirt is not an ornament |
| Bank the US + UK terms as a $1–$2 premium | Give away your single rarest feature for free |
| Review prices after 60 days and once you pass ~25 reviews | Race the cheapest listing in the search results |

---

## 90-day launch plan

| Phase | Price | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| Days 1–30 | Recommended price **minus 20%**, shown as a launch offer with the full price struck through | Buy velocity and first reviews |
| Days 31–60 | Full recommended price | Establish the real price while reviews accumulate |
| Day 60 | Review Etsy Stats: impressions, conversion, favourites | Decide what to hold, raise or drop |
| Days 61–90 | Raise any listing converting above ~3% by $0.50–$1.00 | Find the ceiling without losing conversion |

Once a listing has **25+ reviews and a 4.8+ rating**, test the ceiling figure in the table above — the data shows reviewed listings sustaining $8–$12 in these categories.

---

## Caveats

- These are prices observed on live Etsy listings at the time of research; the Christmas niche reprices hard in **October–December**, when discounting across the category intensifies.
- Prices are **USD**. Many of these buyers are UK/EU, so a strong USD price converts less favourably for them — dual US/UK terms partly offsets that.
- Sales volume data is not public. Review counts are used here as a proxy for how well a listing is selling, which is imperfect but the best available signal.
